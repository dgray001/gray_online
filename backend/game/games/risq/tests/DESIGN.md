1. **orders and actions** (`risq_tests/orders`): receipt order (refunds first), clear previous, cancel, carry-over across turns, validation of every order type, behavior and gather-point actions, rejected actions, update ids and replay.
2. **frontend shape** (`risq_tests/shape`): `ToFrontend` key sets per entity as the frontend expects, fog never leaks hidden units, orders or resources to other players, viewer versus player views.
3. **determinism and invariants** (`risq_tests/invariants`): identical normalized state across repeated runs and permuted submission order; after each turn resources non-negative, population within cap, each entity in exactly one zone or garrison, health and stamina in range, ownership consistent.
4. **ai unit** (`risq_ai_tests/unit`): `ParseModel` for every shipped config, parsers reject bad input, conditions, counters, vars and expressions against hand-built views, target scoring, action resolution to orders.
5. **ai + engine sims** (`risq_ai_tests/sims`): 2-3 AI players on tiny custom maps with tiny purpose-written AI configs and known starts, 1-3 turns. One game checks many things: aibridge view respects fog, AI actions become legal orders, build, hire, gather and attack paths fire, `StopAi` leaves no goroutines, `Results` is sane. Assertions are structural, not exact, since AI goroutine timing varies.
