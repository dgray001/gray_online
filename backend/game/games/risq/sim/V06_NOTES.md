# v0.6: the generalist (work in progress)

v0.6 (`config/ai/v0.6.json`) is generated by `sim/gen_v06.py`, which is the source of truth: edit the generator, run
`python3 gen_v06.py`, never hand-edit the JSON. It is meant to play reasonably from any map, start or game state,
and to beat v0.5 everywhere.

## Where it stands

The latest evaluator (`inputs/eval.json`, 10 maps x 30 games to turn 100, seats swapped every other game) against v0.5:

| | v0.6 | v0.5 |
|---|---|---|
| crushes / crushed | 3 / 50 | 50 / 3 |
| report leads / trails at turn 100 | 199 / 48 | 37 / 210 |
| kills / lost per game | 69 / 67 | 67 / 69 |
| villager idle / VC idle | 1.9% / 28.5% | 6.0% / 38.7% |
| overkill stamina | 50.1% | 27.7% |

The run completed all 300 games with zero errors. V6 is ahead most often on ring, default, triangle, gold and rich;
it is weakest on rectangle (25 crushes by v0.5), sim_close (11), and sim_wide (13). `sim_army` had 15 v0.6 leads,
11 ties and 4 trails; seed 106009 ran 100 turns with no fight.

The economy is solved for now: nonstop villagers to ~50, no idle VC early, production buildings when income can keep
them busy, techs well before turn 50. What is left is fighting (see "Open problems").

## Principles for the generalist

1. **Everything follows from the game state, nothing from the turn number or the map.** Wants are computed each turn
   from what we have and see (income, bank, producers, the enemy we remember), so the same script plays a normal
   start, a rich start, an army-only start or a mid-game state.
2. **Tactics live in the script; the AI code only gives general primitives.** No "raid" or "army" Go actions. When the
   script can't express something, add a small general primitive (an expression input, a counter, a filter, an option)
   and agree it first. The `army` Go action is not used.
3. **Look up costs, hardcode only stats.** Every cost comes from `unit_cost_*`, `building_cost_*`, `tech_cost_*`.
   Unit stats the script can't look up (production rates, damage per hit, rough tech benefits, villager worth in a
   fight) are hardcoded in one place with a comment.
4. **Fix the earliest big mistake first.** Replay a game (`go run . -debug <name>`), read the log and the script's own
   variables (`ai ai-N: vars {...}` every turn), find the first thing that goes wrong, fix it generally, re-run. Don't
   flip settings back and forth to see what happens, and don't trust a theory (including the user's) until the log
   shows it.
5. **Measure with units built and the evaluator, not snapshots.** Units alive mixes production with losses. Judge a
   change with the 300-game evaluator, not one game.
6. **Nothing idles that doesn't have to.** Villagers and the VC should have ~0 idle stamina; a builder is held back only
   for a build that fires this turn and can be paid for; resources should be exhausted (finish nearly empty nodes,
   renew farms at 0).
7. **Investments must be sustainable.** A production building is built only when spare income (current income plus
   bank over 15 turns, minus everything existing producers already eat) keeps it busy; the bar drops as the economy and
   bank grow. A building judged worth it is funded like production, over a few turns.
8. **Never take a bad fight outside the base.**
   - Raids harass the economy only: they never engage soldiers, avoid soldiers, defensive buildings and enemy barracks,
     leave as soon as a soldier is within two spaces, and pause after a party is caught.
   - The army attacks only when the soldiers at home beat the remembered enemy (with its growth and techs) by 1.3x;
     before contact it runs home when outnumbered locally; once in contact it fights on unless the enemy there is
     actually stronger (running in contact just gets it cut down).
   - Only the base is defended regardless of numbers.
9. **Villagers fight or run, never stand and die.** Every villager within a space of enemy soldiers either fights
   together (when they crush the soldiers and enemy villagers there two to one) or takes cover, however few the enemy.
   After a fight they go straight back to work (`resume`).
10. **Every unit always has an owner.** A soldier that leaves the army or the raid is sent home whatever it was doing;
    stale orders are how units got slaughtered without fighting back.
11. **The AI only knows what the player knows.** The engine must not leak information through order validation
    (gather capacity is only enforced where the player can see it, and at execute).

## How the script is laid out (`gen_v06.py`)

- **State and memory**: counts, techs, `rate` (what a villager gathers per turn), remembered enemy units by type
  (`eB/eP/eH/eV`, fading), `ePowSeen` (strongest enemy army seen, growing with their known production buildings,
  fading 2%/turn), `eTech`/`myTech`.
- **Wants**: unit counter values vs the enemy mix, sustainable barracks/Redoubt (`u_*` vs `bar_*`), villager goal
  (income to fill the army room in 15 turns, capped at half the population ceiling), houses (4 turns of production,
  urgency), farms (food workers not covered by wild food within 2 spaces), smith and techs (once the projected army
  repays them).
- **Rates and buckets**: per-resource continuous rates (production, housing upkeep, farm renewal) are funded first,
  together with planned buildings; speculative saving gets a guaranteed share; spare workers split by need; food work
  capped to farm slots plus nearby wild food. One gather bucket per resource.
- **Spending** in priority order: Farming (8 villagers), villagers, the builds that fire (one table, `BUILD_FIRES`,
  drives both the build rules and the builder reserve), techs, mercenaries, units (barracks queue piercers only for
  their share of the need).
- **Military**: raid bucket (a share of blunts), army bucket (every other soldier), home defence, scouting, army-only
  fallback; villagers under attack last.

## AI DSL and engine additions this session

- Expressions everywhere, `min`/`max`, `set_var` (per turn / `persist`), `var()` with built-ins named after counters
  (`population_13`, `enemy_units_visible_13_within_2_of_raid`, `building_count_23_complete`, `unit_cost_food_1`, ...),
  `population_max`, `target_distance(_home)`, `target_health`, `target_max_health`.
- Anchors for `within`: home, a bucket's center, the picker's `target`, and the `unit` a condition is checked for.
- Target picker on `attack`/`move` (`targets`, filters, `score`, `min_score`, `together`, `order` unit/zone/space) and
  `distribute {damage, overkill, queue}` to share attackers over targets.
- `unit_when` on any action: a condition checked per candidate unit.
- `attack` `resume`: queue each attacker's job behind the attack; units already fighting are left alone.
- `enemy_buildings_known_<ids>` now really filters by building id (it used to count every known building).
- Gather picker finishes nearly empty nodes; gather capacity no longer leaks hidden information (receivable on what the
  player knows, cancelled only when the player sees the node full or gone, enforced at execute with slot holders first).
- Map scripts: `player_starts` takes `starting_bank`. New maps `sim_rich` (rich start) and `sim_wide` (radius-2
  player areas, farther apart). Stone is placed identically on every map; start farms removed.
- Debug replays log every script variable each turn (`ai ai-N: vars {...}`).

## The evaluator

`go run . -quiet eval` (about 12 minutes). A config with `buckets` runs `games_per_bucket` games on each bucket's map
(`swap_seats` alternates seats); a plain config is one sim. Every run writes one `outputs/<name>/results.json`
(overall stats per AI plus every sim's stats and games) and `report.txt`. Game seeds are `seed + bucket*1000 + game`,
so any game can be replayed alone as a plain `-debug` scenario with that seed and the right seat order.

Analysis helpers in `sim/tools/`: `engagements.py` (fights, committed soldiers, losses, overkill %), `villagers.py`
(what villagers do each turn), `raid_trace.py` (unit positions and orders).

## Open problems (in rough priority order)

1. **Assault launch regression.** In representative rectangle, close and wide losses, `assault` stayed zero for the
   whole replay, so the army never left home. `ePowSeen` stores poor-vision unit counts as unidentified power and can
   exceed what the army can beat; `homePow` also collapses when units are no longer within one space of home.
2. **Combat efficiency.** V6 overkill is 50.1% versus v0.5's 27.7%; army movement and target spreading still need work.
3. **Army cohesion.** The army bucket can split across spaces and meet the enemy piecemeal.
4. **Finishing games.** Many leads at turn 100 are not crushes; the army needs a safer way to attack defended spaces.
5. **Scouting/raiding.** Scouting is three-phase (home, center, self); no-building starts skip home and can remain in
   center phase because no enemy building can ever be found. Raiding is explicitly gated by `has_eco`.
6. Smaller: stone and gold over-bank late; renew cost is hardcoded because there is no `renew_cost_*` lookup.
